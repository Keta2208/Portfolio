Overview

A consumer-behavior analysis of more than 48,000 customer feedback entries. The work isolated what drives satisfaction and where service is at risk, then turned the findings into something teams could act on every week.

How it was built

  1. 1

    Explore the feedback

    Python exploratory data analysis across 48,000+ entries to isolate satisfaction drivers, service-risk patterns, and behavioral trends.

  2. 2

    Shape the roadmap

    The findings directly informed the organization's service improvement roadmap.

  3. 3

    Package the findings

    Research outputs became Tableau dashboards and concise briefing documents.

  4. 4

    Make them usable

    Feature teams used them to prioritize roadmap work and track retention KPIs, which cut time-to-insight in weekly reviews.

Why it mattered

  • Raw feedback is hard to act on. Turning it into drivers, risk patterns, and trackable KPIs gave teams a shared, quick view each week.
