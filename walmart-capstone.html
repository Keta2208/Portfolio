Overview

The capstone is sponsored by Walmart and asks how agentic AI can support retail demand and inventory decisions. Walmart can't share internal data, so the project runs on public data, built around the M5 Forecasting dataset. The end goal is decision support: forecasts plus supporting context that help a planner decide what to do about inventory.

The workflow

The approach

  1. Start from public data

    Walmart can't share internal data or sponsor paid tools, so the whole project is built on public sources.

  2. Forecasting layer

    M5 store, product, sales, calendar, and price data drive the numerical forecast.

  3. Context layer

    Customer reviews and Walmart financial reports are searched with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). They supply evidence for decisions and are kept out of the forecasting table.

  4. Classify demand types

    Each product's demand pattern is typed with ADI and CV², which separates steady sellers from intermittent ones.

  5. Forecast with uncertainty

    Candidate models are compared with cross-validation, and quantile forecasts express how uncertain each forecast is.

  6. Turn it into decisions

    The forecast supplies the numbers and the RAG layer supplies context, and an agent combines them into decision support.

Data and methodology work

One focus area inside the team project.

  • Data preparation and structured EDA: size, granularity, time coverage, and the product and store hierarchy.
  • Sales patterns, price behavior, and missing values.
  • A shared data map and data dictionary, plus the scope, KPIs, and training, validation, and evaluation periods.
