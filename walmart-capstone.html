The overview

This is the IDS 560 capstone for Group 4, sponsored by Walmart. The team is building an agentic AI approach to support demand and inventory decisions in retail.

The work is split across a team. Known workstreams so far are a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) component, a system architecture diagram, and a dashboard. Phase dates and statuses below are marked to confirm until they are filled in.
